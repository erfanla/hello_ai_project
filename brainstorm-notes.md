## The core idea

A **low-pressure invite app** for friends.
You post an activity ("Burger tonight at 8? 🍔"), and friends simply answer **yes** or **no**.
There is no long group-chat discussion.

**The problems it solves:**
1. People don't ask in the group chat because they fear hearing "no", or they don't want the pressure of a chat.
2. In a group chat, everyone comments and changes the plan, and the person who suggested it ends up sad.
   **Promise: your plan stays your plan.**

## Main ideas (grouped)

### Answering an invite
- Yes/No buttons right in the notification, so you answer without opening the app.
- You can change your answer later.
- **Invite deadline** (e.g. "answer within 1 hour"). After the deadline, no answer = no. *(Mary)*
- **Last-chance reminder** 10 minutes before the deadline.
- **Silent no:** the inviter only sees who said yes. Nobody sees a "no". *(Mary)*

### Making the plan (inviter controls it)
- The inviter chooses how flexible the plan is: fully locked, or with options.
- **Multiple options with a preference:** "burger first, sushi is OK". If enough people (e.g. 3) want sushi, the plan switches.
- **Time window:** "between 6 and 9 pm". Friends pick a time inside the window.
- **Auto-pick:** at the deadline, the app picks the best time/option from the "yes" answers ("7:30, sushi wins!"). *(Mary)*
- **Locked plan + "make your own":** others can't edit your invite; if they want something else, they post their own invite. *(Mary)*
- After people say yes, they arrange details in the app or move to a messenger.

### Money and mood
- **Fun status:** "in the mood today", "short on money this week".
- When friends see "low money", they suggest free plans (dinner at home, sharing food).
- **"Today's food is on me"** option on an invite.
- **Cost tag on every invite:** Free / $ / $$ / $$$. *(Mary)*
- Friends can pay the share of a friend who is short on money. *(Erfan is unsure, see open questions)*

### When nobody says yes
- Go alone and **share a photo** of the food, so friends see what they missed (FOMO).
- **Soft result screen:** never show "0 people". Show "Nobody is free tonight 🌙" plus a "Try again tomorrow?" button. *(Mary)*

### AI features (link to my career goal 🤖)
- **Activity suggestions:** the app learns which activities get the most "yes" answers in the group, and privately suggests similar ones to a person who keeps hearing "no". They post it as their own idea.
- **Smart timing tip:** "Your friends say yes most on Friday evenings." *(Mary)*

### Other
- **Presence score:** accepting invites raises a score showing you are an open, "present" person. *(Erfan worries it may add pressure)*

## Dangers found (Reverse Brainstorming) and fixes so far

| Danger | Fix found so far |
|---|---|
| Friends join but never invite or answer ("ghost members") | *not flipped yet* |
| Nobody checks notifications or updates status, so the app dies | *not flipped yet* |
| Too many features make the UI confusing | **UI must be very simple**; every feature easy to reach |
| Spam notifications, so friends mute the app *(Mary)* | Max invites per day + quiet hours |
| "Pay for a friend" is awkward (shy friend; others don't want to pay) | *not flipped yet* |
| Drama: I said "no" to you in private chat, but the app shows I said "yes" to another plan | *not flipped yet* |
| Privacy: people don't want others to see their yes/no | Answers visible **only inside that invite** *(Mary)* |
| Group drama: part of the group wants to go out without one member | *not flipped yet* |
